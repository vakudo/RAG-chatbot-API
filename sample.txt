The Aurora Project is an internal initiative started in March 2024.
Its goal is to reduce data pipeline latency from 45 minutes to under 5 minutes.
The project lead is Maria Chen, and the team consists of 6 engineers.
The chosen technology stack includes Apache Kafka, Flink, and ClickHouse.
The first milestone, real-time ingestion, was completed in June 2024.
The total budget allocated for the Aurora Project is 1.2 million dollars.
